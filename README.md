# Liangshan: 108 Stars

An original browser strategy game set in the world of *Water Margin*: gather the 108 heroes in the marsh stronghold of Liangshan, win the people's favour and the Emperor's Edict, and bring down the corrupt Grand Marshal Gao Qiu.

Unofficial, non-commercial fan project and a spiritual successor to the strategy games of 1989; not affiliated with Koei Tecmo. Names of people and places come from the public-domain novel; all rules, text and art are original. By evilgouki.

Status: design and engine in progress. See `PLAN.md` and `STATUS.md`.
